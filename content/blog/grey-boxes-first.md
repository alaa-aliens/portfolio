There's a meeting every designer has sat through. You show a screen you've spent a week structuring, and the first comment is about the shade of blue. The structure, the thing that actually decides whether people can use it, never gets discussed.

My fix is boring and it works: nobody sees a colour until we've agreed the structure in grey boxes.

## What that looked like on Trackhawk

[Trackhawk](/work/trackhawk) tracks fleets: trucks, trailers, generators, anything with a GPS unit. When we redesigned it, we agreed everything in lo-fi first. Five core screens, one page of notes, all on one Figma page, for approval before any colour.

Live tracking went first, because it's the page everyone opens the app for. If we got that one right, the rest would follow its rules.

## Write down why

Next to the wireframes sat two benchmark boards, each with a simple borrow / adapt / skip verdict on every pattern we looked at. One product for its clarity, one because the team said it was "much like us".

That board did more for the project than any mockup. Every decision had a reason written next to it, so when someone asked "why is the unit list on the left?", the answer wasn't my taste. It was on the board.

## Why grey works

- **It removes the easy opinions.** Everyone has an opinion about colour. Fewer people have one about information hierarchy, so grey boxes make the room talk about the hard stuff.
- **It's cheap to throw away.** Nobody gets attached to a grey box. Moving a section in lo-fi takes a minute. Moving it after the visual design is a week.
- **It shows what's missing.** Without colour and imagery to hide behind, a screen with no clear main action looks exactly like that.

## Then colour

Once the structure was agreed, the visual design moved fast: thirteen hi-fi screens including dark mode and sign-in, with orange spent on exactly one meaning, "live, right now". The colour decisions were easy because they had a job to do, not a gap to fill.

> Grey boxes are not a stage you rush through. They're where the product gets decided.
